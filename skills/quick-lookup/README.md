# Quick Lookup

Get a fast synthesis for "what do I know about X?" across your KB.

## Output shape

- `WhatKnown`
- `Evidence`
- `UncertainOrMissing`

## Example prompts

- "What do I know about Acme's renewal?"
- "What do I know about Jane Doe?"
- "Give me a quick lookup on our Stripe migration."
- "Summarize what I know about this topic from notes and contacts."
