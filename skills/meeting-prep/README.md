# Meeting Prep

Create a complete pre-call brief for a person or company using your KB data.

## Output shape

- `WhoTheyAre`
- `RecentInteractions`
- `ContextSignals`
- `SuggestedAgenda`
- `QuestionsToAsk`

## Example prompts

- "Prep me for my meeting with Jane Doe tomorrow."
- "Build a pre-call brief for Acme Inc."
- "What do I know before this investor check-in?"
- "Give me a concise meeting brief for this contact."
